We had all 30 Google Chirp 3 HD voices read a support call full of easy-to-mishear details (a spelled code, a phone number, a date, a time, an amount and a name) in India's most spoken languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi, the mother tongue of about 89% of Indians (Census 2011). Google's speech recogniser wrote down what it heard, once from clean audio and once through an 8 kHz phone line like our calls, and we counted the characters it got wrong compared with the other voices. Lower is clearer. Kore was the clearest female voice and Iapetus the clearest male voice of the 30 across these languages, so these are the two voices we use.
