# Website audio

`sample-call.mp3` is the website's sample screening call (components/site/sample-call.tsx). It was generated for this website on 2026-10-22 as one continuous two-speaker recording with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, model `gemini-2.5-pro-tts`, English (India), multi-speaker: the AI hiring agent is the `Kore` voice, the product's own female voice, and the candidate is the `Leda` voice, a different female voice. A style prompt asked for a real, relaxed phone call with natural pace and short pauses between turns. No real person's voice or call is used, and the page says the voices are generated. The words are the script in components/site/sample-data.ts; each turn's text and start time come from a Deepgram (nova-3, en-IN) transcript of the recording. The one overlap, the candidate cutting in at 0:32, was mixed afterwards: her line starts 0.35 s before the agent's would end, and the agent fades out under her over 0.25 s. The rest of the take is untouched. Encoded mono, 64 kbps, with lame.
