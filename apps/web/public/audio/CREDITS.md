# Website audio

`sample-call.mp3` is the website's sample screening call (components/site/sample-call.tsx). Both voices were generated for this website with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (Chirp 3 HD, English (India)) on 2026-10-21: the AI hiring agent is `en-IN-Chirp3-HD-Kore`, the product's own female voice, and the candidate is `en-IN-Chirp3-HD-Gacrux`, a different female voice so the two speakers are easy to tell apart. No real person's voice or call is used, and the page says the voices are generated. The words are the script in components/site/sample-data.ts, synthesized line by line with the exact transcript text; each line's start time in the script comes from the assembly. The two overlaps (the candidate cutting in at 0:35, and the goodbye at 1:12) were mixed on purpose to sound like a real call.
