# Bulk calling ("Call selected (N)"): design so far

> **STATUS: NOT APPROVED. NOT PART OF THE INTEGRATION PLAN. DO NOT BUILD FROM THIS DOCUMENT.**
> This is slice A2 as it stood in `architecture-draft-v6.md`, moved out by the lead decision of 2026-10-03 (`02-resolutions.md`, "Round 7 (lead decision)"). It is kept so the work is not lost. It must be designed again and reviewed on its own, starting from the open objections in section 3, before anything here is built.

Date: 2026-10-03. Research and writing only: no code, config, migration or test was changed. Labels as in the drafts: **FACT** (source), **RECOMMENDATION**, **ASSUMPTION**, **unverified**. `JAVA/` = `apps/api-java/src/main/java/desk/`, `MIG/` = `packages/db/drizzle/`.

**What the product does instead (v7).** A call starts exactly as it does today: a person clicks Call for one candidate, through the existing endpoint and its existing guards. Imported candidates appear on the job's board ready to call. Results flow back automatically (v7 §6.1).

**Why it left the integration plan.** Six review rounds did not converge: majors went 17, 12, 9, 4, 1, then back up to 5, and 4 of the 5 round-6 majors were about A2. A2 is a calling-engine feature (a dispatcher, capacity control across tenants, hosted-agent lifecycle) that grew inside the integration plan; each fix added machinery that the next round found new holes in. It needs its own design pass with telephony capacity, provider behaviour and the legal check as first-class inputs.

---

## 1. The problem it solves

- **FACT (problem).** Without it, every imported candidate still needs one person's click to be called (Round 1 CR-5, `02-resolutions.md` R1.10). For a pilot importing 200 people from an ATS, that is 200 clicks, which undercuts "connect once".
- **FACT (constraint).** Fully automatic calling of imported candidates waits for a written founder decision on consent, calling hours and DND (`apps/api-java/MIGRATION_STATUS.md` line 373). A queued batch confirmed by one person was A2's middle ground; whether such a batch counts as an "auto-dialer" under TRAI TCCCPR is **unverified** (https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf).

## 2. The design as of v6 (unreviewed after round 6)

Everything below is v6's text, condensed. Where round 6 found a problem, section 3 says so.

### 2.1 User flow

- **Where:** the job's candidates bulk bar gets "Call selected (N)", plus "Select everyone not called yet" (the redesign spec asked for a bulk call there, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-saas-redesign-design.md` line 202) and "Select everyone who didn't connect". Needs `calls.initiate`. Disabled with a reason when the job has no agent.
- **Route:** `POST /jobs/{j}/calls/queue` with `{candidateIds: [...]}` (at most 100) or `{select: 'not_called' | 'not_connected'}`, resolved on the server inside the insert transaction (FACT: the board loads at most 200 per page and the bulk route takes at most 100, `JAVA/hiring/HiringController.java` lines 211-233, 362-389). `dryRun: true` returns the true count. `not_connected` = latest outcome a failure or `no_conversation`, except `invalid_number`, `line_not_connected` and `dial_uncertain`. Both filters leave out people closed or removed in an ATS.
- **Dialog:** one `ConfirmDialog` with the count, calling hours, the remaining monthly budget and a **lower bound**, never an estimate: "At most 2 queued calls at a time in this workspace and 50 calls a day, including calls you place yourself. 200 people take at least 4 days; calls may wait when lines are busy across HRVoice." (FACT: defaults of 3 concurrent phone calls and 50 a day, `JAVA/platform/Env.java` lines 132-133; "Never fabricate a number", `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-app-consistency.md` section 8.)
- **Confirm prepares the call setup:** the request runs the existing `provision` for the job under the version lock before writing rows, so the first unattended dial does not create a hosted agent (up to 10 s connect plus 30 s read, `HostedVoiceAdapter.java` lines 46-47).
- **Board:** "Queued to call (37 left, next call after 10:00 tomorrow)", "Stop queued calls" (one UPDATE to `cancelled`), skipped or waiting rows with their reason, a per-row "Queued to call" tag with "Remove from queue". A person's own Call on a queued person dials as usual, and the queued row is later skipped as "Called by hand".

### 2.2 Rows and dispatcher

- **Rows:** one `scheduled_calls` row per selected candidate with a phone and no open row: `reason = 'bulk'`, `attempt = 1`, `due_at = now()`, `agent_id` = the job's agent at confirm time (kept only because the column is NOT NULL), `requested_by_user_id`. Every status change sets `updated_at = now()` in the same UPDATE (FACT: `updated_at` has a default and no trigger, `MIG/0025_call_cost_ledger.sql` line 110). Skip or wait reasons as `bulk:<code>`. No schema change (FACT: `status scheduled|dialing|done|cancelled|skipped`, one open row per candidate, `MIG/0025` lines 95-136). Slice 0 would have made `UseCase` validation refuse `callPolicy.retry.enabled: true` while nothing dispatches retry rows (FACT: `apply` inserts retry rows when that flag is on, `VoiceSessions.java` lines 572-596; it is `false` today, `usecases/hiring.json` line 136).
- **Dispatcher:** one daemon dial thread, woken by the click and otherwise by its own 15-second timed wait (not `@Scheduled`). Each pass visits orgs with open bulk rows (in-memory set) and claims at most one due row per org, jobs taking turns, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, sets it to `dialing` and commits before dialing. It dials only while the org's live phone calls are below `max(1, cap - 1)` (one slot kept for a person's own click) and the platform's bulk calls are below `VOICE_BULK_MAX_CONCURRENT`.
- **Per row:** rebuild the clicker's `Actor` from live membership (active user, active workspace); skip if they lost `calls.initiate`, the row was cancelled, the candidate is closed in the ATS, or the candidate got a phone call after the row was created ("Called by hand"); wait until the calling window opens (FACT, `callPolicy.retry.window` 10:00-19:00 Asia/Kolkata, `usecases/hiring.json`; no Sundays, `CallOutcomes.nextAttempt` lines 89-108); dial with the job's **current** agent (FACT: `dialLocked` refuses when the agent differs from `jobs.agent_id`, `VoiceSessions.java` lines 401-404); call the existing `startOutbound`; on success set `done` and `voice_session_id`.
- **Stable error codes.** Today every refusal is `ApiError.conflict(message)` with code `conflict` (FACT, `VoiceSessions.java` lines 362-463, `ApiError.java` lines 54-56, `Db.java` lines 103-115, `BudgetGuard.java` lines 50-54). A2 gave each a code, keeping HTTP 409:

  | Code | Raised | Row becomes |
  |---|---|---|
  | `calls.concurrency_cap`, `calls.agent_in_use`, `calls.busy` (lock timeout, or retrieval failed) | before any session row | `scheduled`, `due_at = now() + 60 s` |
  | `calls.provider_busy` (HTTP 429, or a refusal body saying the account is at capacity) | after the session rows, which `dialLocked` deletes through the delete policy | `scheduled`, +60 s |
  | `calls.daily_cap` | before any session row | `scheduled`, next calling window |
  | `calls.provision_failed` | before any session row | `scheduled`, +5 min, `bulk:provision_failed` |
  | `calls.not_ready` (line not connected, agent not published, no agent), `budget.exceeded` | before any session row | this row and every other open bulk row of the job `skipped` |
  | `validation_failed` (phone), `calls.destination_not_allowed`, `calls.live_call` | before any session row | `skipped` |
  | `calls.dial_failed`, or anything else | possibly after the session row | `done` or `skipped`; never dialed again |

- **At most once.** A row that reached `dialing` is never set back to `scheduled` unless its code says the dial was not placed. Crash recovery: a row left `dialing` for more than 10 minutes becomes `done` with the newest phone session for that candidate created after the row's `updated_at`, otherwise `skipped` ("interrupted"). On shutdown the thread stops claiming and waits up to 60 s (`SmartLifecycle`).

### 2.3 Dial classification and provider capacity

- **Transport decides "placed".** No reply, a timeout, an I/O error or a 5xx is `network` (may have been placed); any 4xx or `success: false` body means not placed. App-only state `dial_uncertain` ("We lost contact while placing this call. They may have been called; ask before calling again.") for `failed` + `network` + no conversation id, kept out of the "didn't connect" shortcut. FACT why: `dial` passes both a `success: false` message and any exception text to `CallOutcomes.fromDialFailure`, which falls through to `unknown` (`HostedVoiceAdapter.java` lines 253-272; `CallOutcomes.java` lines 37-47).
- **Shared provider capacity.** Every workspace dials through one provider account and one number (FACT: one `voiceApiKey` and one `voicePhoneNumberId`, `HostedVoiceAdapter.java` lines 47-49, 253-256). FACT: "Free 4 concurrent calls; Starter 6; Creator 10; Pro 20; Scale 30; Business 40" (https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/agents); calls over the limit "are rejected with an error, unless call queueing is enabled" (https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/guides/burst-pricing). Three measures:
  1. **A capacity refusal is not a dial.** `provider_busy` for an HTTP 429, or a `success: false` body without a conversation id matching `concurren|too_many|system_busy|capacity` (FACT: the provider documents `too_many_concurrent_requests` and `system_busy`, https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/19571824571921-API-Error-Code-429; the outbound-call reference documents only a 422, https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/integrations/twilio/outbound-call). `dialLocked` then deletes the `voice_sessions` row it just inserted and its `agent_sessions` row, through a new migration: `GRANT DELETE ON voice_sessions TO platform_app;` plus `CREATE POLICY vs_delete_unplaced ON voice_sessions AS RESTRICTIVE FOR DELETE USING (status = 'pending' AND external_conversation_id IS NULL);` (FACT: `MIG/0014_elevenlabs_rls.sql` revokes DELETE; `vs_session_fk` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, `MIG/0013` line 66; `vs_tenant_isolation` is permissive `FOR ALL`, so the restrictive policy narrows deletes). Audit `voice.session.provider_busy`; message "All call lines are busy right now. Try again in a minute."
  2. **A platform ceiling on bulk dials,** `VOICE_BULK_MAX_CONCURRENT` (default 2), from in-memory per-org live counts read in each claim transaction; people's own clicks are not limited by it. Set to the plan's concurrency minus the slots kept for clicks (proposed: half).
  3. **Burst stays off:** `agentBody` never enables it; a test asserts it (FACT: burst is "configured per agent" at "double the standard rate", same burst page; default unverified).
- **Destination allowlist** `VOICE_DIAL_COUNTRY_CODES`: in v7 this moved to item G as a pre-existing risk; a bulk row would be `skipped` on `calls.destination_not_allowed`.

### 2.4 Hosted agents

- **One hosted agent per job, without a migration.** Today one hosted agent per agent version is re-synced whenever the job context changes (FACT, `VoiceSessions.provisionLocked`, lines 154-219, always `environment = 'test'`). A2 gave each job its own deployment row through the existing unique index `vpd_version_provider_env_unique (agent_version_id, provider, environment)`: `environment = 'job:<jobId>'` for phone calls, `'test'` for browser tests. FACT: `environment` is free text with no CHECK (`MIG/0013` lines 24, 72-74); Nest reads and writes only `'test'` (`apps/api/src/providers/elevenlabs/voice-session.service.ts` lines 259-268, 726-733); "There is no limit to the number of agents you can create on any plan" (https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/agents). The advisory lock stays per agent version.
- **Retrieval failure refuses the dial** with `calls.busy` before any re-sync (FACT: a failed retrieval returns no chunks and changes the hash, `JAVA/rag/Retriever.java` lines 76-89; `CallContextCompiler.java` lines 147-160, 248).
- **Same-job guard:** before re-syncing a job's own hosted agent, refuse with `calls.agent_in_use` if a `pending`/`active` session on that slot has a different `provision_snapshot.contextHash`.
- **Idle stale job agents deleted at the provider** by the 5-minute loop (old version, closed job, or a company document deleted since the slot's last dial), keeping the row with `external_agent_id = ''`. **Round 6 found this unsafe (section 3, items 2-3).**

### 2.5 Background work that came with A2

- **Background reconcile for every org** in the 5-minute loop: phone sessions with a conversation id, ended 3-60 minutes ago with no transcript and no failure reason, or still `active` past `voiceMaxPhoneMinutes + 2`; at most 5 per org per run, 40 s budget; provider deletes after it with at least 15 s.
- **The 5-minute loop always runs** its database step, with the provider steps only while voice is on (FACT: `expireStale` returns at once when voice is off, `VoiceSessions.java` lines 679-680).
- **Scheduler pool** `spring.task.scheduling.pool.size: 3` plus a test tying the pool to the number of `@Scheduled` methods (FACT: Boot's scheduler "uses one thread by default", https://docs.spring.io/spring-boot/3.4/reference/features/task-execution-and-scheduling.html; `JobQueue.poll` runs jobs inline, `JobQueue.java` lines 87-90).
- **Heartbeats and health:** an in-memory `lastRunAt` per loop; `oldestQueuedCallMinutes`; public `/health` `degraded` when a heartbeat is older than 3 intervals or a lag passes 30 minutes; the numbers in a log line.
- **`AUTOMATION_PAUSED`** stopping the dial thread and the loop's provider steps; after a restore, open bulk rows cancelled with `bulk:restored`; erasure cancelling the person's open rows with `bulk:removed`.

### 2.6 Definition of done and live checks (v6)

Pool size and its test; the loop's fixed order with voice off; the connection-pool check; heartbeats; `AUTOMATION_PAUSED`; `VOICE_BULK_MAX_CONCURRENT` set from the provider plan; tests for the delete policy, provider busy, retrieval failure, per-job slots, idle agents, current agent and crash recovery; one supervised live batch across **two jobs**, 3-5 calls to team members' own numbers, plus one deliberate over-capacity attempt that logs the provider's real refusal shape.

### 2.7 Why not the provider's batch-calling API

FACT: the provider offers batch calls, one agent per batch, with a default concurrency of "the minimum of either 50% of your workspace's concurrency limit or 70% of your agent's concurrency limit", and "Zero Retention Mode (ZRM) cannot be enabled for batch calls" (https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/phone-numbers/batch-calls; API https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/batch-calling/create). Rejected in v6: our per-dial checks (budget, caps, allowlist, closed in the ATS, called by hand) and webhook matching need our own session row and conversation id per dial. Operating rule proposed: no dashboard batch calls on the production account.

### 2.8 Founder decision 10a and capacity (v6 text)

Ship "Call selected (N)": one person's confirmed click queues calls for the chosen people within calling hours, each dialed at most once; legal check on "auto-dialer" status and on number or header registration (unverified). Capacity is shared across workspaces. **Platform capacity** ≈ `VOICE_BULK_MAX_CONCURRENT` × calling-window minutes ÷ minutes a call holds a slot. ASSUMPTION: about 5 minutes including ring time. With the default 2 and the 10:00-19:00 window: 2 × 540 ÷ 5 ≈ 216 bulk calls a day for every workspace together; on Pro (20 concurrent) with a ceiling of 10, about 1,080.

## 4. Before this is designed again

1. Gate P done, with the hosted adapter's live calls working (v7 §13.1).
2. Item G done (v7 §13.1, founder decision 15): who may place calls at all is settled before anyone can place 50 a day with one click.
3. Live checks on a test account: the provider's and Exotel's real refusal bodies at capacity; what deleting an agent does to its conversations and audio; when a call's configuration binds; several agents through one number on the Exotel route.
4. Founder decision 10a restated with the real capacity inputs (provider plan **and** Exotel channels), and the legal check on "auto-dialer" status, number or header registration, and a per-candidate call frequency.
5. A design that plugs into v7 as it stands: every dial still through `dialLocked` (with the closed-in-ATS block); provider HTTP only on the integration lane with a per-run budget; results flowing back through v7's delivery rows unchanged, because a batch call is an ordinary call once placed.
