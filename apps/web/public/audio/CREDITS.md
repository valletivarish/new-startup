# Website audio

`sample-call.mp3` is the website's sample screening call (components/site/sample-call.tsx). Both voices, the AI hiring agent's and the candidate's, were generated for this website with ElevenLabs text-to-speech on 2026-10-02. No real person's voice or call is used, and the page says the voices are generated. The words are the script in components/site/sample-data.ts; each line was checked against the script with speech-to-text. The two overlaps (the candidate cutting in at 0:33, and the goodbye at 1:07) were mixed on purpose to sound like a real call.
